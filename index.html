FAQ

Good Questions

What languages do you host in?

English and Mandarin, fluently. I can host fully in one language or switch seamlessly between both, which works especially well for weddings and events with guests across generations.

What is your hosting style?

High energy, warm, and audience-focused. I read the room and adapt: playful and interactive when the crowd wants fun, polished and sharp when the occasion calls for class. My job is to make your guests feel part of the event, not just watch it.

Do you travel outside Singapore?

Yes! I'm happy to host destination weddings and overseas events. Travel arrangements are discussed during your consultation.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can, especially for weekends and peak seasons like year-end D&D and wedding dates. Most clients book 1 to 3 months ahead, but if your event is around the corner, reach out anyway and I'll tell you honestly if I can make it work.

Is a deposit required?

A deposit secures your date, with the balance due after the event. Full details are confirmed during your consultation, so there are no surprises.
